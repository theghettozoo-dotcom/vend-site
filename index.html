The system

Four moves, every round.

Trading fees fund a basket of nine real prizes. When the pot covers a prize, one holder wins it through a public draw. The team stakes its supply; items won are sold for buyback and burn.

01

Trade

3% of every $VEND trade: 2.7% fills the pot, 0.3% goes to Souvenir.

02

Fill

A slot unlocks once the pot covers its price +10%.

03

Draw

A public drand round picks one holder, weighted by balance.

04

Return

Items won are sold in the store; proceeds buy back and burn $VEND.
